Classic Mechanistic Competitions

SN2 vs. E2 (Bimolecular Rivalry)

Both require strong reagents. SN2 wins on methyl/1° substrates with unhindered nucleophiles. E2 wins on 3° substrates, with bulky bases (KOtBu), or at high temperatures.

SN1 vs. E1 (Unimolecular Solvolysis)

Both proceed through the exact same carbocation intermediate formed in the slow rate-determining step. Moderate temperature favors SN1; heat favors E1 due to favorable entropy.
