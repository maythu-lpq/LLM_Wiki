Mechanistic Decision Logic

This prediction follows the exact decision algorithm from wiki/index.md:

  1. Substrate Sterics: Methyl is strictly SN2; 3° substrates completely block SN2 (E2 with strong base, SN1/E1 with weak base).
  2. Base Strength vs. Nucleophilicity: Bulky bases (KOtBu) always enforce E2 (Hofmann product). Good nucleophiles that are weak bases (I⁻, RS⁻, CN⁻) enforce SN2.
  3. Temperature (Entropy): Elevated temperature favors elimination (E1/E2) over substitution because $\Delta S^\circ > 0$.
  4. Solvent: Polar protic promotes carbocation ionization (SN1/E1); polar aprotic keeps anions unshielded, favoring SN2.
